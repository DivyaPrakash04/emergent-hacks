How the score works

The same four steps run for every room. The formula is plain arithmetic, so every number in the deck can be checked by hand. Open “Show the math” on any card to see it.

  1. Filter out deal-breakers

    A room stays out of the deck if it goes past any walk-away limit or lacks a “Must” facility. When the deck runs out, these rooms appear with the one change that would bring each back.

  2. Score each factor out of 100

    At or better than your ideal earns 100. Exactly at your walk-away limit earns 0. In between, the score drops in a straight line.

    100 × (limit − value) ÷ (limit − ideal)

  3. Turn importance into weights

    Each slider is divided by the total of all sliders, so the weights add up to 100%. Maya’s 8, 6, 4 and 2 become 40%, 30%, 20% and 10%.

    weight = slider ÷ sum of sliders

  4. Add up and deal the deck

    Multiply each factor score by its weight and add. The highest score is the first card; a tie goes to the cheaper room. Swiped rooms leave the deck.

    score = Σ (weight × factor score)

Facilities score the share of points a room has: Nice is worth 1, Important 2. A furnished room you marked Important, without the gym you marked Nice, gets 2 of 3 points, so 67. Dates score the days of your stay a room doesn’t cover, with 0 as the ideal and your gap tolerance as the limit. Trust counts only while “Prefer verified hosts” is on: the score is multiplied by 0.85 when the host isn’t fully verified (for example, landlord consent pending), and by 0.7 for a listing from outside Roomsure.
